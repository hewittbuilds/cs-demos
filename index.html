Which ball is heavier?

Guaranteed two-weighing strategy

  1. Weigh 3 balls against 3 balls.
  2. If they balance, the heavy ball is one of the 2 left out. Compare those two.
  3. If one side is heavier, the heavy ball is among those 3 balls. Compare any two of them. If one is heavier, you found it; if they balance, the third is the heavy ball.
